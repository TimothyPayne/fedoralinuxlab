# Current Project

## Purpose

Help people in New Mexico build practical AI and computer-literacy skills.

## Audience

The main audiences are students, job seekers, and adults returning to education, especially people with limited formal education.

## Initial Content

- AI basics
- Hands-on AI practice
- Computer basics
- Linux operating-system skills and problem-solving practice
- Internet safety and privacy
- Job skills
- Free computer-literacy education links

More researched resources can be added over time.

## Constraints

- Make the site accessible.
- Protect visitor privacy.
- Check facts and links for accuracy.
- Keep the work manageable with limited time.
- Use low-cost hosting.
