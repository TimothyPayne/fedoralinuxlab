AI Practice Lab · AI words, in plain language

Artificial intelligence (AI)
Computer systems that perform tasks such as recognizing patterns, working with language, or making predictions.
Generative AI
AI that creates content, such as text, pictures, audio, or video.
Chatbot
A program you talk with through messages. Some chatbots use generative AI.
Prompt
The question, instructions, or information you give an AI tool.
Follow-up
Another message that adds details, asks a question, or requests a change.
Context
The information available to the AI for its current response, such as instructions and parts of the conversation.
Model
The trained mathematical system that an AI tool uses to produce results.
Training
The process of adjusting a model using examples or other feedback. An ordinary chat does not necessarily retrain the model.
Token
A small unit a model uses to process information. In text, it may be part of a word, a whole word, or punctuation.
Large language model (LLM)
A model trained on large amounts of text to work with language, including generating responses.
Hallucination
An AI response that includes invented or incorrect information, even when it sounds confident.
Bias
Patterns that can lead an AI system to treat people or ideas unfairly or give a one-sided result.
Verification
Checking a result against reliable sources or real-world evidence before relying on it.
Automation
Using software or machines to carry out steps with less hands-on work.
AI agent
An AI system that can use tools and take steps toward a goal. Its actions and permissions still need human oversight.
