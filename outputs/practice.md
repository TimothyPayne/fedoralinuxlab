### Practice Page Copy

# Practice AI Skills

Practice asking questions, improving prompts, and working through short AI exercises.

You do not need to be a computer expert to begin. Choose a practice project, try one step at a time, and save the work you complete. Your saved practice can help you remember what you learned and describe your experience later.

## Start with a guided exercise

- **Quick start:** Learn how to ask AI questions step by step.
- **Better questions:** Use a one-page reference for improving questions to AI.
- **Short practice:** Try short exercises for using AI at different skill levels.
- **Office practice:** Work through exercises related to office administration.
- **Family-friendly practice:** Explore practice intended for people ages 10 and up.

## Use a patient learning approach

You can start a question with this instruction:

> Act as a patient tutor. First rephrase my question more clearly if it is unclear, then answer step by step in plain language, and end by asking me one good follow-up question so we can keep improving the answer together.

This can help turn a one-time answer into a conversation. Read the answer carefully and decide whether it makes sense before using it.

## Build a practice prompt

Enter a topic or assignment, choose the kind of help you want, and describe your answer. The practice prompt builder can create text that you can copy or share with a chatbot for feedback, suggestions, and a next challenge.

You can choose:

- Student coaching, coding, image generation, writing help, or research
- An age or education level
- Gentle, standard, or stretch practice
- Short feedback, a table, or step-by-step coaching

Do not enter private or sensitive information into a chatbot or practice form.

## Why practice helps

Practice gives you a place to try questions, review answers, and improve one step at a time. Saving your work also gives you examples of what you have practiced. The goal is steady learning, not perfection.

More practice projects will be added as they are produced.
