How do people make robots dance?

Boston Dynamics explains that its Spot dance routine was carefully choreographed and programmed. The robots were following synchronized timing rather than listening to the music. Human creativity and engineering made the performance possible.

Experiment on a test computer

A test box is a spare computer you can afford to reset. It gives you room to experiment with software or an operating system while keeping everyday work on your main computer.

Use made-up data and copies, keep important files backed up elsewhere, and understand how you would reset the machine before changing it. A spare computer can still lose files or expose accounts; it is not automatically isolated or protected. Check what commands and permissions do before accepting them.

Learn about a test computer and Lubuntu for older equipment. Watching videos or asking ordinary AI questions does not require another computer.

Why can almost-human robots feel unsettling?

The “uncanny valley” describes the discomfort some people feel toward almost-human robots or animated characters. You can prefer a practical machine shape; a robot does not need a human face to be useful.
