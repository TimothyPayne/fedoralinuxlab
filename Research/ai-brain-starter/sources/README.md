# Original sources

Copy in two or three useful files for your first job. For example: a project brief, some audience notes and a piece of your own writing.

Use copies you are comfortable sharing with your AI provider. Leave passwords, customer records and confidential documents out of the starter folder.

Keep these originals unchanged. Add a new dated file when something changes, then ask your AI to update the relevant working notes and drafts.
