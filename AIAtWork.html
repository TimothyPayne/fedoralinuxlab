Meeting notes become next steps.

Before · rough notes

Team meeting: Jo checks the supplies Friday. Sam books the room Monday.

→
After · sample result
  • Jo: check supplies by Friday
  • Sam: book room by Monday

Something to try

Make an action list from these meeting notes. Include who does each task and when: Jo checks the supplies Friday. Sam books the room Monday.

Compare the sample result with the notes. Don’t let the AI add people, tasks, or dates.

1. Try these rough notes

Open your AI chat, then copy or type this request. Need help getting started?

Turn these notes into a short email to my team: Thursday meeting moved to 2 pm. Same room. Bring one idea for improving our filing system.

2. Look at the answer

Did it keep Thursday, 2 pm, and the same room? Did it invent a room number or a reason for the change?

3. Ask for a change

Make it friendly and professional, under 60 words. Add a clear subject line. Keep only the facts from my notes.

Sample email · illustrative result

Subject: Thursday meeting at 2 pm

Hi team, our Thursday meeting has moved to 2 pm in the same room. Please bring one idea for improving our filing system. Thank you!

4. Check before using it

Does it sound like something you would send? Check the details and edit anything that doesn’t fit. An AI draft is yours to review.

You’ve finished your first practice journey.

After you have checked the revised message against the notes and corrected anything that doesn’t fit, you’re done. You don’t need to send the practice email.

You have asked a question, checked the answer, added useful details, and reviewed the result. Those are steps you can use again.

ANOTHER WAY TO PRACTICE · ABOUT 5 MINUTES

Describe it. Make a simple drawing.

You can use words to ask AI for an image. Start with a small desk drawing that could fit a practice worksheet or a presentation.

1. Try this prompt

Open one of the image tools below. Choose its image-creation option, paste these words, and select Create or Generate.

Create a simple black-and-white line drawing of a small desk with a laptop, notebook, pencil, and coffee cup. Use clean outlines, a plain white background, and no shading or text.

2. Look at the result

Can you find all four objects? Are the outlines clear? Check for extra objects, unwanted letters, or shading. Ask for a correction if something does not fit.

3. Try one easy variation

Make the same drawing, but replace the coffee cup with a small plant. Keep the clean black-and-white outlines and no shading or text.

What to look for: the desk, laptop, notebook, and pencil stay; a plant replaces the cup. Compare the pictures before you use one.

Use a made-up scene while practicing. Before using an image at work, check your workplace’s AI rules and the service’s terms.
