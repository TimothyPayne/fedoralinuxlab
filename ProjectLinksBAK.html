This is Why People Find Robots Creepy:

Uncanny Valley is a concept that explains why people sometimes feel uncomfortable around robots or animated characters that look almost human, but not quite. As a robot becomes more human-like, people usually find it more appealing. However, when it looks very close to a real person but still has small differences—such as unnatural facial expressions, stiff movements, or lifeless eyes—people often react with Uneasiness or Even Fear. This dip in comfort is called the Uncanny Valley
